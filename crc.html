Why does this work?

The sender: a generator with r + 1 bits needs r check bits. Append r zeros to the data, divide using XOR, and replace those zeros with the remainder. The resulting frame divides evenly by the generator.

The receiver: divide the entire received frame by the same generator. Do not append extra zeros. A nonzero remainder detects an error; a zero remainder means no error was detected.

CRC does not correct errors. A zero remainder can also occur for some changed frames. The undetected example demonstrates this limitation.

Generator as a polynomial

Each 1 includes a power of x; each 0 omits it. The leftmost bit gives the highest power.
